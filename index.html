Processing Integrity

The Quality Standard

From single-origin harvest to nitrogen-sealed maritime shipping, our four-tier quality protocol guarantees cargo stability across extended ocean transit.

PROTOCOL 01

Direct Orchard Traceability

Direct contract farming across verified micro-climates in California, Chile, Kashmir, and Izmir ensures consistent kernel moisture, caliber, and natural oil balance.

PROTOCOL 02

5-Stage Optical Sortex

High-definition monochromatic and infrared optical cameras inspect every nut individually, eliminating discolored pieces, shell fragments, and micro-defects.

PROTOCOL 03

Controlled Humidity Storage

Consignments are maintained under constant atmospheric humidity (<55%) and temperature (12°C–14°C) to eliminate rancidity and preserve natural crunch.

PROTOCOL 04

Consignment Lab Clearance

Every shipment is accompanied by batch-certified aflatoxin analysis, microbiological reports, and official government phytosanitary clearance documents.
